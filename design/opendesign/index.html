My devices

Connect your harnesses without sharing account passwords. Each device gets its own revocable credential after you approve a matching code in this browser.

Connect through your harness

Give these instructions to Pi on the device you want to connect
  1. Run the device login on the machine that hosts your harness.
  2. The harness prints a short device code and starts polling.
  3. Enter the code here and approve it. The harness receives its own credential; your password never leaves this browser.
pi-share team login acme-eng --scope reviews
# opens a device code; approve it at https://pi.acme.internal/devices
